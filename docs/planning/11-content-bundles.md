# 11 — Content Bundles

---

## 1. Overview

Content bundles are the primary way learning content is organized and access-gated. There are two types:

1. **Dictation Exercise Bundles** — organized listening/transcription exercises
2. **Skill Practice Drill Sets** — structured IELTS practice sets

Both follow the same access model:
- A curated selection of **free bundles** is always available to Freemium users (no Sparks needed)
- **Premium bundles** require either Sparks (one-time unlock, permanent) or a Premium subscription

## 4. Permanent Unlock Model

When a Freemium user spends Sparks to unlock a bundle or set:
- The unlock is **permanent** — they keep it forever, even if their Sparks balance drops
- The unlock is **account-bound** — it does not transfer to another account
- If they later upgrade to Premium, Premium covers all bundles and sets automatically (the Sparks-unlocked ones are still visible as "unlocked" in their history)

---

## 5. Bundle Access in the Focus Bar System

Each dictation exercise costs **1 Focus**.  
Each practice drill set costs **1 Focus**.  
Free bundles and premium bundles cost the same Focus — access tier only affects whether you can enter the bundle at all.

See [04-focus-bar.md](./04-focus-bar.md) for the full Focus system.
