# 06 — Achievements and Titles

---

## 1. Achievement System Overview

Achievements are permanent records of accomplishment displayed on the user's profile. They are grouped into 11 categories with tiered progression (like Minecraft — many achievements, always something to discover).

**Total achievements: 150+**

Many achievements have tiers: Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum → Legendary, each requiring progressively more effort.

**Secret achievements** are shown as `???` in the achievement list — a faint silhouette of the icon is visible, but the title, description, and Sparks reward are hidden. Tooltip: *"Complete hidden conditions to reveal this achievement."* Character stands next to the mystery card with a curious expression.

## 3. Milestone Reward System

In addition to achievements, there are major progress milestones — checkpoints that trigger celebration screens and title upgrades.

| Milestone | Condition | Reward |
| :--- | :--- | :---: |
| Welcome Package | Complete placement test | 100 Sparks + Newcomer title |
| First Week Champion | Log in 7 days in the first 7 days | 150 Sparks + Persistent title |
| First Month Finisher | Complete 30 days of any activity | 500 Sparks + badge |
| CEFR Skill Up | Mastery profile detects consistent skill improvement | 300 Sparks + updated CEFR display |
| League Promotion (each) | Pass qualification test | Sparks + exclusive cosmetic (see [07-leaderboard-and-leagues.md](./07-leaderboard-and-leagues.md)) |
| 1,000 Cumulative Sparks Earned | Lifetime earned Sparks hits 1,000 | 50 Sparks bonus |
| 10,000 Cumulative Sparks Earned | Lifetime earned hits 10,000 | 200 Sparks bonus |
| 100,000 Cumulative Sparks Earned | Lifetime earned hits 100,000 | 1,000 Sparks bonus |
